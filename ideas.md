# Design Exploration — AI Career Intelligence Platform

## Chosen direction — Signal Ledger

### Design Movement

**Swiss editorial design meets contemporary research tooling.** The site should read like a concise case study from a high-caliber product and engineering organization: wide margins, decisive type, typographic hierarchy, sharp rules, and data visualizations treated as primary storytelling devices.

### Core Principles

1. **Evidence over embellishment:** every visual detail should amplify the proof points, workflow, or technical credibility.
2. **Asymmetric editorial rhythm:** sections stagger content across a flexible 12-column canvas rather than centering every element.
3. **Human context, systems clarity:** the product turns fragmented career information into an intelligible decision-making tool.
4. **Intentional contrast:** near-ink surfaces, pale paper, and luminous signal accents make hierarchy instantly readable.

### Color Philosophy

The page will use **soft paper** as the primary canvas to signal clarity and trust, a near-black **ink** for authority, a muted cobalt for interface-grade information, and **signal lime** as the unmistakable ownable action and outcome color. Lime is reserved for validated, positive movement—match quality, performance gains, and key numbers—so it never becomes visual noise.

### Layout Paradigm

An **editorial field guide**: the hero anchors left in a generous asymmetrical lead column while a vertical systems panel sits at right. Subsequent sections alternate between tall annotation rails, wide content landscapes, proof-point cards, and data strips. The structure should feel like stepping through a tightly edited technical report rather than scrolling through generic marketing blocks.

### Signature Elements

1. **Signal lines:** fine cobalt rules and chart paths that connect outcomes to the corresponding workflow stage.
2. **Evidence tags:** small all-caps labels such as `BENCHMARK / ILLUSTRATIVE` and `SYSTEM / ONLINE` that clarify information provenance.
3. **Number tiles:** oversized metrics arranged in compact, offset panels with lime indicator dots.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should behave like a professional research console: understated, responsive, and never theatrical. Navigation links and cards use crisp color shifts and 1–2 px translated movement; contextual details reveal only when they help a reader orient or evaluate the work.

### Animation

Elements enter with short opacity and upward-translate reveals (180–360 ms, custom ease-out) in reading order. Data paths may draw once on entry; metric indicators pulse once, gently. Hover states use transform and opacity only, under 180 ms. All nonessential animation is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System

**Instrument Serif** handles the major case-study headlines, bringing editorial confidence and a human, career-oriented cadence. **DM Mono** is used for labels, metrics, and technical annotations. **Manrope** supports body copy and UI controls. Headings use low line height and intentional line breaks; labels are uppercase and tightly tracked.

### Brand Essence

**A career intelligence operating system for builders who want evidence, not guesswork.**

Personality: **precise, pragmatic, quietly ambitious.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and specific; CTAs invite inspection instead of applying pressure; microcopy acknowledges uncertainty honestly.

Examples:

> “Turn every job description into a decision you can defend.”

> “Inspect the signal, then decide what to build next.”

### Wordmark & Logo

Use a **split-field signal mark**: two offset vertical brackets containing a rising, segmented path. It suggests both structured extraction and career progression while remaining legible as a standalone app icon. The wordmark uses a custom treatment of the platform name, with `CAREER / SIGNAL` separated by a slim rule.

### Signature Brand Color

**Signal Lime — #C7FF3D.**

## Metric Integrity Note

All numerical outcomes on this website are **illustrative benchmark targets**, not verified production results. They must be replaced with instrumentation, evaluation, test, and deployment records before publishing the claims as factual.
